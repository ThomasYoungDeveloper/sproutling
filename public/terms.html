Community rules

Chat, usernames, guild names and trades are shared with other players. When you use them:

  • Be kind. No bullying, threats, harassment, hateful words or mean names.
  • Keep personal info private. Don't share your or anyone's address, school, phone number, social media or other apps.
  • Keep it clean. No sexual content, nothing violent or graphic, and no spam.
  • Play fair. No cheating, hacking, exploiting bugs on purpose, or selling accounts or pets for real money.
  • Report problems. Press and hold a message to report or block someone. We review every report.

If you break these rules, we may remove content, turn off chat for your account, or suspend or delete your account, depending on how serious it is.
