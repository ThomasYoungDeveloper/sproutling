Sproutling Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Sproutling is a game where your healthy habits help a pet grow. It is made by Thomas Young, TY Creative ("we"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: we only use what the game needs, we never sell your information, and there are no ads or tracking.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and the username you choose. Your email is used to sign you in and to send password-reset and confirmation emails. It is never shown to other players.
  • Your game: your pets, eggs, items, coins, streaks, and the daily habit totals you log (steps, water, sleep and similar), so your progress is saved and works on every device you sign in on.
  • Apple Health (only if you allow it): the phone app can read your daily steps, sleep, water, workouts, exercise and mindful minutes, and activity rings. These are used only to give in-game rewards, such as XP, warming eggs and finds on walks. We save daily totals as part of your game progress, not your detailed health records. You can turn Health access off at any time in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Sproutling.
  • Notifications (only if you allow them): a notification token for your phone, so we can let you know about friend requests, gifts, trades, battles and guild news.

What other players can see

Friends and guild members can see your username, your pets and what they're wearing, your streak, badges, Pet-dex count, step totals for guild goals, and whether you were active today. They never see your email or your Apple Health details.

How we use it

Only to run the game: saving your progress, playing with friends and guilds, and sending the emails and notifications described above. We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell or rent it, and Apple Health data is never shared with anyone or used for marketing.

Services we use

Sproutling runs on a few trusted services that store or send data for us: Supabase (accounts and saved games), Vercel (the website), and Expo (building the app and delivering notifications). Apple and the App Store handle installing the app. These services only process data to provide Sproutling.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the game: open your account (the round button at the top right) and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your login, saved game, friends, trades and guild membership. If you led a guild, it passes to the longest-standing member. You can also ask us to delete it by email.

Children

Sproutling is meant for players 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

Your data is sent over encrypted connections, and each player can only read and change their own saved game. No system is perfect, but we work to keep your information protected.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be announced in the game.
