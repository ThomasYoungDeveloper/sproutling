Sproutling Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

See also our Terms and community rules and Help and support.

Sproutling is a game where your healthy habits help a pet grow. It is made by Thomas Young, TY Creative ("we"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: we only use what the game needs and we never sell your information. The phone app shows a few optional ads; see Ads below.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and the username you choose. Your email is used to sign you in and to send password-reset and confirmation emails. It is never shown to other players.
  • Your game: your pets, eggs, items, coins, streaks, and the daily habit totals you log (steps, water, sleep and similar), so your progress is saved and works on every device you sign in on.
  • Apple Health (only if you allow it): the phone app can read your daily steps, sleep, water, workouts, exercise and mindful minutes, and activity rings. These are used only to give in-game rewards, such as XP, warming eggs and finds on walks. We save daily totals as part of your game progress, not your detailed health records. You can turn Health access off at any time in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Sproutling.
  • Notifications (only if you allow them): a notification token for your phone, so we can let you know about friend requests, gifts, trades, battles, chat messages and guild news.
  • Chat messages: messages, quick phrases and stickers you send to friends and your guild. They're kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically. If someone reports a message, we look at that message and the chat around it to keep the game safe.
  • Help and support messages: anything you send us from Help and support in the game, along with your username and app version, so we can reply and fix problems.
  • Crash reports: if the app hits an error, it sends us the error message and the app version so we can fix it. Crash reports don't include your health data or messages.

What other players can see

Chat is only with friends you've added and your own guild, never strangers. Friends and guild members can see your username, the messages you send them, your pets and what they're wearing, your streak, badges, Pet-dex count, step totals for guild goals, and whether you were active today. They never see your email or your Apple Health details.

How we use it

Only to run the game: saving your progress, playing and chatting with friends and guilds, keeping chat safe, answering support messages, fixing crashes, and sending the emails and notifications described above. We do not sell or rent your information, we never share your game or account details with advertisers, and Apple Health data is never shared with anyone or used for advertising or marketing.

Ads

The phone app shows a small ad at the bottom of some screens, and you can choose to watch a short ad for an in-game bonus (up to 3 a day). Ads are never shown on the home screen, during the intro, in battles or in chat. Ads come from Google AdMob, which receives your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), your rough location from your internet address, and how the ads are used. We don't send AdMob your email, username, game data, chat or Apple Health data.

  • Adults: iPhone asks whether Sproutling may track you. If you say yes, ads can be more relevant to you. If you say no, you still see ads, just not personalized ones. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Players 13 to 17: only see non-personalized, teen-appropriate ads and are never asked to allow tracking.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's advertising policy.

Services we use

Sproutling runs on a few trusted services that store or send data for us: Supabase (accounts and saved games), Vercel (the website), Expo (building the app and delivering notifications), and Google AdMob (ads in the phone app, as described above). Apple and the App Store handle installing the app. These services only process data to provide Sproutling.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the game: open your account (the round button at the top right) and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your login, saved game, chat messages, friends, trades and guild membership. If you led a guild, it passes to the longest-standing member. You can also ask us to delete it by email.

Children

Sproutling is meant for players 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

Your data is sent over encrypted connections, and each player can only read and change their own saved game. No system is perfect, but we work to keep your information protected.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be announced in the game.
