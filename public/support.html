Common questions

My steps aren't showing up. Open iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Sproutling and make sure Steps is turned on. Then open Sproutling again. Steps update when the app opens.

How do I add a friend? Go to Social › Friends and share your friend code or invite link, or type their code or trainer name into "Add a friend".

How do I report or block someone? In chat, press and hold their message and choose Report or Block. Blocking hides you from each other in chat, friends and battles.

Can I turn chat off? Yes. Open your account page and set Chat to Off, or to Phrases & stickers only.

My pet was knocked out. Every pet heals 10% an hour on its own, up to half health. Use a potion to heal faster.

How do I delete my account? Open your account page and tap Delete account at the bottom. This removes everything and can't be undone.
